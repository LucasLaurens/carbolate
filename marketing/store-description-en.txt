Carbolate estimates the carbon footprint of your browsing, live, with no account and nothing ever sent outside your browser.

Every web page has a cost: data crossing the network, a machine doing the work, a screen kept on. That cost is real, but invisible. Carbolate makes it visible, gram by gram, while you browse.


HOW IT WORKS

Carbolate follows each page for as long as it stays open, not just at load time: videos, images loaded as you scroll and background requests all count. Three sources are measured:

• Network: the bytes actually transferred
• Device: how long the page took to load
• Screen: how long the page was actually on screen

All of it is converted into grams of CO₂ equivalent (gCO₂e) using published factors (ADEME, IEA).


YOUR DAY AT A GLANCE

• A daily counter that climbs as you browse and resets at midnight
• A clear grade: Sober, Moderate, Intensive or Excessive
• A concrete equivalent: emails, hours of streaming, kilometres by car or by plane
• Where you stand against an average day of browsing (120 gCO₂e, ADEME 2023 / The Shift Project 2022)
• Your grade for the day right on the toolbar icon


WHAT PAGES REALLY LOAD

• Page loads, requests and data transferred
• The share of requests that came from third parties
• The CO₂ saved by the browser cache
• Your 5 heaviest sites of the day


NEW: YOUR TREND

• Your last 7 days, one bar per day in the colour of its grade
• The change against the previous week
• History kept for your choice of 1 day, 7 days or everything
• CSV export of your whole history, and if you want, an automatic export before anything is deleted


PRIVACY

• 100% local: everything stays in your browser
• No account, no server, no tracker, no network request
• Even sorting requests into third-party or not happens inside the page: only counts are stored, never the sites met
• Interface in English and French


AN HONEST NUMBER

Carbolate measures what a browser can actually observe. The power drawn by servers and by your machine cannot be read from a web page, so it is estimated from published averages. Manufacturing of servers, network gear and your device is excluded: the figure is a floor, about 20 to 25% below a full assessment. The extension says so, rather than showing false precision.


LIMITATIONS

• Internal browser pages (chrome://, about:, file://) are not measured
• Tabs opened before installing must be reloaded to be measured
